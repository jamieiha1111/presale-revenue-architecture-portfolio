Business context

A confidential luxury residential development in Honolulu carried a $500M+ total sellout inventory. Construction financing was locked behind a single milestone: 60% of gross sales value under executed contract. That threshold wasn't optional — it was the capital gate.

Performance conversations inside the organization were centered on unit velocity. But the financing threshold was dollar-denominated. That mismatch — units trending acceptably, dollars lagging materially — was creating a blind spot at exactly the wrong time.

Core risk identified: At prior run rate, achieving the 60% financing threshold required approximately 10x acceleration in dollar-based absorption. Fragmented reporting was masking this exposure from executive stakeholders.

Why this matters

Situation

A $500M+ development was reporting stable unit velocity while dollar-based absorption lagged the 60% lender financing threshold — a distinction that wasn't visible inside existing reporting frameworks.

Task

Identify and quantify the pacing gap, model the capital timeline risk, and surface it to executive stakeholders — in a way that changed behavior, not just awareness.

Action

Built a cumulative GSV pacing model against the lender threshold schedule. Quantified a ~10x acceleration gap. Diagnosed funnel-stage conversion decay. Elevated capital risk to leadership. Informed channel reallocation. Led strategic repositioning analysis that shifted the organization's narrative from prestige to livability and long-term value.

Result

Reframed the organization's performance conversation from "demand problem" to "misaligned value proposition." Influenced marketing reallocation, messaging pivot, and executive risk awareness. Delivered measurable engagement lift and established a structured revenue pacing framework that didn't exist before.
